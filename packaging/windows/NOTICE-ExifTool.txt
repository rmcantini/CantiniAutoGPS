ExifTool (bundled)
==================

This folder contains exiftool.exe and the exiftool_files folder, which are the unmodified
Windows build of ExifTool by Phil Harvey, downloaded from https://exiftool.org (the only change
is renaming "exiftool(-k).exe" to "exiftool.exe", as the ExifTool installation notes describe).
